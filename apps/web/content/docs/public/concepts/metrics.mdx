**Metrics** (`/metrics`) aggregates operational signals — run success rates, workflow throughput, agent activity, and connector health summaries — for day-to-day monitoring.

## What Metrics covers

Typical panels and charts include:

- **Run volume and outcomes** — success, failure, cancellation over time
- **Workflow performance** — top workflows by executions or error rate
- **Agent utilization** — invocations and outcomes where tracked
- **Connector health** — sync errors and degraded integrations (summary view)
- **Time ranges** — last 24h, 7d, 30d, custom where supported

Exact widgets depend on your tier and connected data.

## Metrics vs Enterprise cost attribution

| | **Metrics** (`/metrics`) | **Enterprise → Cost** |
|---|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Audience** | All operators | Enterprise admins |
| **Focus** | Operational health | Spend, budgets, attribution by team |

Enterprise customers use both: Metrics for reliability, Cost tab for finance alignment.

## Metrics vs History

**Metrics** shows trends and aggregates. **History** (`/audit`) shows immutable event lines for compliance and investigation.

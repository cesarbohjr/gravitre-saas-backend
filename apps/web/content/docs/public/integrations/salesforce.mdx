Query and update Salesforce records from workflows and agents.

**Auth:** OAuth with PKCE

## Setup

1. Open **Connectors → Salesforce → Connect**.
2. Complete OAuth in your Salesforce org.
3. Confirm connected status before scheduling production workflows.

## Tips

- Test SOQL-based steps in dry-run first
- Use **Environments** to separate sandbox vs production org connections

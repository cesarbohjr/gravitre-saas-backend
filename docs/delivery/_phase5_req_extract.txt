   genuinely trained versus heuristic, must be a real, live computation 
   over real BusinessOutcome and Module A records, never a separately- 
   maintained number that could silently drift out of sync with the 
   actual, current state of the system.

   as well: if any report or dashboard metric would realistically be 
   expected to show real variance over time (a success rate, an 
   engagement number, a confidence score) and instead shows suspiciously

   measurement periods, flag this as a real, direct finding of the exact 
   same failure class, just manifesting in a report rather than a 
   workflow output.

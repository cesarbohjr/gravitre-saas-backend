# Positioning: approval-gated AI operator (July 2026)

## Claim

**Your AI operator asks before it acts, shows its work, and never guesses silently.**

This is a defensible product claim against shipped surfaces — not aspirational marketing.

## Mapped surfaces

| Claim phrase | Shipped surface |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Asks before it acts | Approval / confirm gate on connector writes; chat execution panel; `/approvals` |
| Shows its work | Tool chips, source links, **View audit trail** → `/audit`, verified result payloads |
| Never guesses silently | Assumption notes / inferred fields labeled in execution results; advisory-only recommendation cards (STA-314) |
| You stay in control | In-app one-pager `/ai/help/control` + docs `/docs/concepts/control` |

## What we do **not** claim

- Autonomous “just do it” CRM writes without confirmation
- Silent field invention presented as fact
- ML recommenders trained on synthetic data

## Pricing / packaging note

If sales or pricing copy still describes a shakier “AI that just does it” era, align to this framing. Engineering does not change Stripe plans in this pass — copy and narrative only.

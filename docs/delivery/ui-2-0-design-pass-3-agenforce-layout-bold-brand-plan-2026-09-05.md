## A. Brand tonality (logo-led)

### What the logo teaches

- **Weight:** Thick dual-bar mark, not hairline topology.
- **Contrast:** Emerald field vs white/black mark; black mark on warm grey — always punchy.
- **Geometry:** Parallel strata with forward slant — motion without fluff.
- **Flat confidence:** No soft glow-as-identity; no pastel intelligence haze as the brand voice.

### Reject (current “too faint” failure mode)

- Low-opacity violet washes as primary atmosphere
- Whisper borders / barely-there shadows as the only depth
- Muted grey-on-grey type that reads unfinished
- “Mineral soft” as the personality (atmosphere OK only if type + CTAs stay bold)

### Adopt — bold Gravitre system

| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| **Primary** | Saturated Gravitre emerald (logo green) — CTAs, key accents, mark fields |
| **Ink** | Near-black / graphite for headlines (heavy weight) |
| **Canvas** | Warm off-white / light grey (logo grey ground) **or** deep graphite void for Agenforce-like hero — **decide in §B** |
| **Surface** | Solid elevated white (or solid charcoal panels), clear borders — not frosted whisper glass |
| **Secondary A** | Cool signal blue — connectors / systems (supporting, not pastel) |
| **Secondary B** | Amber — approval / attention (status only) |
| **Secondary C** | Deep violet — intelligence **accent** only (sparingly; never the soft wash identity) |
| **Success** | Emerald-bright for verified outcomes |

**Personality words:** bold · clear · operator-grade · simple on the surface · sophisticated underneath.

## D. Messaging hierarchy (authorized)

**Brand:** Gravitre  

**Hero promise:** One AI brain for your entire business.  

**Support:** Connect your tools, teams, data and processes. Gravitre works across your business to automate work, coordinate teams, measure results and continuously improve how your company operates.  

**Primary CTA:** Put Gravitre to work  
**Secondary CTA:** See how it works  

**Benefit line:** Your tools. Your teams. Your data. Working as one.  

**Differentiation (below fold):** It doesn’t just automate the work. It understands whether the work is working.  

**Category (late):** AI Operating System for Business — never the hero lead.  

**Do not lead with:** AI OS · multi-agent orchestration · AI workforce · enterprise automation platform · agentic BI.

### Progressive sections (copy intent)

1. **Hero visual story** — Departments → GRAVITRE (Shared Brain) → Business outcomes; connectors under.  
2. **Problem** — Fragmented AI; needs them to work together.  
3. **One brain** — Every team gets AI; they all share the same brain (permissions respected).  
4. **Pillars** — Work as one · Know what’s working · Get smarter over time · Governed by design.  
5. **Tangible demo** — Outcome request → analysis checklist → issues → recommended actions. **Illustrative only** — no fake pipeline dollar claims.  
6. **Accountability** — Objective → … → ROI/learning loop in plain CEO language.  
7. **Simplification** — Replace AI sprawl with one intelligent layer (connect, don’t rip-and-replace stack).
